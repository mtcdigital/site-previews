The playbook

How to get reviews (the right way)

1Ask in person

At the end of every job, while they're smiling at the result: "If you're happy with it, a quick Google review would really help us out."

2Text the link the same day

Send the direct review link before you leave the driveway. It opens right to the 5-star box. Follow up once after 3 days.

3QR card everywhere

Review cards on invoices, a card on the clipboard, a sticker on the truck. One scan and they're leaving the review.

⚠️ Don't pay for reviews. Google bans offering anything in exchange for a review (discounts, lunch, gift cards). Reviews can get deleted and the profile flagged. Just ask every happy customer, every single time. That's what the top crews do.
Quick wins

Google profile fixes we can knock out together

1
Add your new website to your Google profile

2
Set your service area

List the towns you cover (Eaton Rapids, Charlotte, Mason, Holt, Lansing…) so you show up in those searches too.

3

Today Google only knows "Pressure washing" and "General contractor". Adding "Tree service" and "Fence contractor" puts you in those searches.

4

Before & after shots (like your sunroom job) every week or two. Fresh photos = more trust and more clicks.
