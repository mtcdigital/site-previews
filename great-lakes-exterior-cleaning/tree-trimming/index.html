Tree Trimming FAQs

How much does tree trimming cost near Eaton Rapids?

It depends on the size of the tree, how many branches come off and how easy it is to reach. We give free quotes. Text a photo to (517) 386-0664 for a fast ballpark.

When is the best time to trim trees in Michigan?

Late fall through winter, when most trees are dormant, is usually best for the tree. Oak trees in Michigan should be trimmed between November and March to help prevent oak wilt. Dead or dangerous limbs can be removed any time.

Do you remove branches hanging over my roof?

Yes. Limbs over the roof drop leaves in your gutters, scrape shingles and can fall in a storm. Cutting them back is one of the most common jobs we do.

Do you haul away the branches?

Yes. Cleanup and haul-away are part of the job unless you ask us to leave firewood or chips for you.

Can you trim near power lines?

We do not work on branches touching main utility power lines. Call your utility (Consumers Energy or your electric co-op) for those. We can handle branches near the service line to your house when it is safe to do so. Ask us when you call.

Do you do storm cleanup?

Yes. After a storm, call or text with photos and we will get you on the schedule as fast as we can.
