Fence & Porch Repair FAQs

Do you repair fences or only install new ones?

We repair fences. That includes broken boards and pickets, leaning sections, rotted posts and sagging gates. Fixing the damaged section usually costs far less than a whole new fence.

How much does fence repair cost?

It depends on how many sections or posts need work and the type of fence. We give free quotes. Text photos to (517) 386-0664 and we can usually give you a ballpark fast.

Why is my fence leaning?

Usually a rotted or broken post, or the ground heaving during Michigan freeze and thaw. We reset or replace the post so the section stands straight again.

Can you fix a sagging gate?

Yes. Sagging gates usually need new hinges, a brace or a reset post. We fix it so the gate swings and latches the way it should.

Do you repair porches and steps?

Yes. We replace soft or rotted porch boards, worn steps, loose railings and trim. A safe porch is not a place to wait.

Can you clean my fence or deck after you repair it?

Yes. Since we also do pressure washing, we can wash the fence or deck after the repair so the whole thing looks fresh. Ask for both in one quote.

What other repairs do you do?

We take on general contracting and repair jobs around the house. If it is on your to-do list, call or text (517) 386-0664 and ask.
